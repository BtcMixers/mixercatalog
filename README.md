Frequently Asked Questions

Is using a mixer legal?

The legality of cryptocurrency mixers varies by jurisdiction. In most countries, using privacy tools is legal, but some jurisdictions have restrictions. We recommend consulting with a legal professional in your area.

How do you ensure my privacy?

We employ multiple privacy-enhancing technologies including CoinJoin, confidential transactions, and zero-knowledge proofs. Our strict no-logs policy means we don't store any identifying information about your transactions.

What fees do you charge?

Fees vary between 1-3% depending on the mixer you choose and the level of privacy you require. More complex mixing with longer delays typically costs less than instant mixing.

Can I mix other cryptocurrencies besides Bitcoin?

Yes! Many of our featured mixers support Ethereum, Litecoin, Monero, and other major cryptocurrencies. Check each mixer's specifications for supported coins.
